# 🐠 Live Aquarium

**My Little Ocean** is a cozy, interactive 3D aquarium for kids. Feed the fish, watch them grow, learn fun ocean facts in **বাংলা** or English, and learn to look after pets responsibly.

Built with React, Vite, Three.js, React Three Fiber and drei. Everything runs in the browser: no backend, no accounts, no downloads.

## Features

**Fish and the tank**
- Cute fish built from simple shapes in code (clownfish, blue tang, yellow tang, angelfish, betta, a tall-finned batfish and a pufferfish that blows up into a spiky ball when tapped or scared). No 3D model files are needed.
- Smooth curved swimming, wagging tails, flapping fins and blinking, sleepy eyes.
- A colourful reef: branching, table, bubble, brain, tube and swaying finger corals, plus kelp and anemones.

**🌿 A living seabed**
- Natural sand with current ripples, fine grain, damp patches and soft shade around the boulders, with moving light patterns on top.
- Pebbles, broken shells and bits of bleached coral rubble scattered across the floor (always in the same spots).
- A low wall of natural boulders at the back, green with moss on top and dotted with pink coralline crust.
- Swaying seagrass meadows, see-through sea lettuce, crimson feather algae and grape algae; the red algae and the grape beads glow faintly at night.
- Sponges (an orange barrel, purple vases and blue crusts), half-buried clams that open a crack now and then, and a striped sea snail that hides in its shell when tapped.
- A rippling water surface at the top with light shimmering down, sunbeams that sway, an air stone sending up a steady stream of bubbles, and a bluer haze in the distance.
- A shoal of tiny silver fish swims as one flock far in the background, glints as it turns, and swerves away from the shark.
- Everything is placed in three layers (back, edges, low front) so the middle of the tank stays open for the pet fish, and the detail level drops on phones, in calm mode and on slow devices.

**Feeding**
- **Feed** drops a handful of pellets. **Tap the water** to drop food exactly where you want it.
- Drag a falling pellet to bring it to a shy fish.
- Fish notice food, race to it and eat it with hearts and bubbles.
- No overfeeding: full fish say "I'm full!", and too much food in the water triggers a friendly warning.

**Moods and personalities**
- Each fish is playful, shy, sleepy or snacky.
- Fish are happy 😊, hungry 🍤, curious 👀 (they come and say hi), sleepy 💤 or weak 🥺.
- Tap a fish to open its small card: four ring gauges (tummy, growth, health, love), quick-info chips (home, food, size, lifespan, super power) and a swipeable fact carousel. Every tap makes it do a happy barrel roll.

**Growing up**
- Feeding raises growth and love. Fish get bigger and unlock new colors at 50% and 100% growth.
- A grown-up fish with lots of love has a baby (up to 10 fish).

**Caring matters**
- Fish that stay very hungry lose health, turn pale and slow down. If they wait too long, they die.
- Lost fish are kept in **🌈 Memories** with their name, how long they lived and how many snacks they ate.
- If the tank becomes empty, the child can adopt new fish.

**👧 Asma, the fish's best friend**
- Asma is a kind, cheerful girl who dives into the tank holding her breath, with puffed cheeks, goggles and yellow flippers.
- The fish swim in circles around her while she says sweet things and shares snacks with them.
- She stays as long as her breath lasts (shown as a 🫧 breath meter), then swims up for air.
- Afterwards the fish ask "Bring Asma back!" and the 👧 button glows. Tap it to call her again once she has caught her breath.
- Tap Asma for a twirl and hearts. She also visits on her own every few minutes (this can be turned off in Settings).

**🦈 The cheeky shark**
- Every few minutes a goofy cartoon shark with grumpy eyebrows sneaks in and chases the fish.
- Fish near it panic 😱 and dart away. If the shark reaches one, that fish faints from fright 😵, floats belly-up for a moment and loses a little health. The shark never eats anyone and can never finish a fish off.
- **Tap the shark 3 times** to bonk it on the nose and chase it away. The fish reward you with hearts.
- Asma isn't scared of it: when she dives in, the shark flees.
- It can be turned off in Settings, and Settings also has a **🦈 Call the shark** button.

**🐢🦀🐙 Tank neighbours** (tap one to open its learning card)
- A gentle **sea turtle** glides slowly around the back of the tank and does a twirl when tapped.
- A little red **crab** scuttles sideways along the sand, waves its claws and tidies up leftover food.
- Smiling **starfish** hop and spin when tapped.
- An **octopus** by its den slowly changes colour, waves when tapped, and squirts ink (then turns pale) when the shark comes near.
- Two **jellyfish** pulse and drift in the upper back corners and glow at night.
- A **seahorse** bobs beside the kelp and turns to peek at you.
- Two **cleaner shrimp** potter about the sand, nibble leftovers and backflip when tapped.
- A shy **lobster** lives behind the treasure chest, peeks out, and zooms backwards with a tail flick.
- A **giant clam** slowly breathes open and closed, shows a glowing blue mantle, puffs bubbles, and snaps shut when tapped or when the shark comes near.
- A colony of **garden eels** sways out of the sand and turns to watch the fish. They all dive into their holes when tapped, when a fish swims right over them, or while the shark is around, then peek out again one by one.
- Two **sea urchins** bristle their spines when tapped, and a spotted **sea cucumber** inches slowly across the sand and squishes when tapped.
- A **hermit crab** wanders the front sand with its spiral shell. Every few minutes it finds the other shell lying nearby, climbs out (you can see its soft pink tummy) and moves in. Tapped, it pulls into its shell.
- A **cuttlefish** hovers on the left with a rippling fin skirt and colour clouds rolling over its skin; tap it for a rainbow light show.
- Three neon **sea slugs** (nudibranchs) crawl slowly over the sand: a blue-and-yellow one, a purple one with orange-tipped fingers, and a red "Spanish dancer".
- A spotted **ray** glides low over the sand and now and then buries itself until only its eyes peek out. Tapped, it lifts off in a puff.
- A green **moray eel** peeks out of a cave in the big back boulder, opening and closing its mouth as it breathes.

**🌙 Night friends** (only after dark)
- An **anglerfish** drifts deep at the back with a glowing lure bobbing on its head.
- A little group of **lanternfish** drifts by, dotted with glowing blue lights.
- Tap the water at night and blue **glowing plankton** sparkles where you touched.
- Neighbours stay on the sand, at the edges or behind the fish, so the middle of the tank stays clear.

**🐋 Big sea visitors**
- Every few minutes a **manta ray**, **dolphin** or **blue whale** swims past far behind the reef, one at a time and never while the shark is in. The whale puffs a spout of bubbles.
- Tap a visitor while it passes to learn about it. Turn visitors off, or call one, in Settings.
- **✨ Rare visitors:** now and then (about 1 visitor in 12) an **orca**, a giant **ocean sunfish** or a singing **humpback whale** swims by instead, with a "Rare sighting!" message. If none has come for two days, the next visitor is sure to be rare, and rare guests the child hasn't met yet come first. Their stickers are gold and give 5 pearls.

**🔍 Hide-and-seek**
- Every day a **leafy sea dragon** hides among the kelp or seagrass, and a tiny purple **pygmy seahorse** clings to one of the sea fans, perfectly camouflaged. Their spots change every day.
- Once a day a message says someone is hiding. Still not found after a few minutes? A faint sparkle gives a little hint.
- Finding one gives hearts, 3 pearls and its sticker.

**🤝 Reef partnerships** (with a fact the first time each is seen)
- **Cleaning station:** now and then a cleaner shrimp invites a fish over; the fish hovers still while the shrimp hops on and cleans it.
- **Clownfish** sometimes swim down and snuggle into an anemone, their stinging but safe home.
- The **moray eel** opens wide while its own little cleaner shrimp tidies its teeth.
- When the crab wanders close to the **octopus**'s den, the octopus reaches for it and the crab scuttles off (only playing!).

**🦪 Pearls & decorations**
- Earn pearls by playing: the treasure chest (+1 every couple of minutes), every new sticker (+2), a pearl that now and then shines inside the open giant clam (tap it for +3), hidden friends (+3) and gold rare stickers (+5). Small rewards share a soft cap of 15 a day, so pearls stay special.
- Tap the pearl counter in the top bar to open the **Decorate** shop. Buy decorations and show or hide them any time: rainbow fan coral (5), old anchor (8), diver helmet that puffs bubbles (12), sand castle with waving flags (15), lantern corals that glow at night (18), bubble volcano (25), mossy rock arch the fish swim under (30) and a sunken shipwreck behind the reef (40).
- Every decoration has its own spot, measured so it never lands on a coral, rock or creature. Pearls and decorations are kept when the aquarium is reset.

**📖 Ocean sticker book**
- Tapping a fish or creature for the first time earns its sticker (35 in all, in six sections: pet fish, tank friends, night friends, ocean visitors, gold rare visitors and hidden friends).
- The book shows progress, grey "?" spaces for animals not met yet, and opens each collected sticker's learning card again. Half and full books are celebrated.

**🌗 Day and night**
- The tank follows the real clock by default: day (6:00–17:00), sunset (17:00–19:00) and night.
- At night the water darkens, moonbeams replace sunbeams, sea snow glows like plankton, jellyfish and some corals glow, fish nap more often, and the shark stays away.
- Tap the ☀️/🌅/🌙 badge in the top bar to switch the look, or choose Clock, Day, Sunset or Night in Settings.

**Learning and fun**
- **💡 Facts**: ocean, habitat and fish-care facts in Bangla (default) or English, now with 5 facts for every animal.
- **🪙 Treasure chest**: tap it for coins, sparkles and a surprise fact.
- Bubbly sound effects, synthesized in the browser with no audio files.

**💍 Love letters for Asma**
- The 💍 chip counts the days since the wedding (29 September 2026) and opens a letter from Saimun behind the secret word.
- **Every day is a new letter:**
  - a greeting that fits the time of day;
  - one of 61 notes;
  - a "today's little surprise" tile to tap open (a reason I love you, a promise, a date idea, a tiny mission or a question);
  - a seasonal P.S. and a rotating Bangla line and sign-off;
  - "One more note, please" for up to three bonus notes.
- The pieces rotate at different speeds, so the same full letter doesn't come back for decades.
- It knows the calendar:
  - Jumma, Ramadan and both Eids (from the browser's Islamic calendar);
  - New Year, Valentine's Day / Pohela Falgun and Pohela Boishakh;
  - both birthdays;
  - round day counts (100, 200, 500, 1,000 days…).
- It notices her visits: a letter number, a 🔥 streak, "back again today?" and "I missed you" after a few days away. This is saved in this browser only.
- **Anniversaries are special:**
  - a gold card and a slower line-by-line reveal;
  - a hand-written letter for each of months 1–11 and years 1–5 (later ones are built from rotating paragraphs);
  - a love coupon to claim, or three on a yearly anniversary;
  - three promise cards to flip on yearly anniversaries.
- All the words live in `src/data/loveNotes.js`.

**🎂 Asma's birthday (23 December)**
- From 1 December a 🎂 chip counts down the days; behind the secret word is a sealed envelope with a live countdown.
- On her birthday the fish wear party hats, Asma wears a tiara and balloons float up the tank (tap to pop them).
- The surprise opens with the secret word and plays in four steps: unwrap a present, light the candles and make a wish, blow them out (with a little Happy Birthday tune) and cut a slice, open a jar with one wish for every year of her age, and read a letter from Saimun.
- It comes back every year with her new age, new candles, a new letter and a new jar of wishes. Letters live in `src/data/birthday.js`.

**🎈 Saimun's birthday (31 January)**
- Through January a small blue chip counts down the days to Saimun's birthday; tap it for a reminder. On the day it turns gold and says "Saimun's Birthday!".

**Settings**
- Name tags, sounds, Asma's surprise visits, shark visits, big sea visitors and calm mode (less motion) can be turned on or off.
- Tank time: Clock (default), Day, Sunset or Night.
- You can reset the aquarium. Memories are kept.

Progress (fish, growth, love, health, memories, stickers, settings) is saved in the browser's `localStorage` and survives refreshes.

## Plays great on every screen

The buttons live in a slim glass **rail on the left edge**. Nothing covers the middle or the bottom of the tank, and the camera frames the tank in the space beside the rail, so no fish ever swims behind a button.

| Device | Layout |
| ------ | ------ |
| **Phone (portrait)** | One-row top bar, a slim rail on the left, fish cards and panels slide up beside the rail, toasts rise from the bottom of the free space. The camera steps back a little so the fish keep room to swim. |
| **Phone (landscape)** and **small laptops** | Icon-only rail from just under the top bar; the 💍 and 🎂 chips move to the right and Asma's card to the bottom right. |
| **Tablet** | Same rail with labels, side sheets. |
| **Desktop** | Same layout with gentle hover effects and mouse parallax. |

- **Tuck it away:** the ‹ handle at the bottom of the rail hides it, and the tank glides back to full width. A small 🍤 › tab brings it back, and the choice is remembered.
- **Quiet mode:** after 20 seconds without a touch, the buttons fade softly so the tank takes the whole stage, and they come straight back on the next touch or mouse move. This doesn't happen while a card or panel is open.
- The rail's glass follows the time of day (warm at sunset, deep blue at night), and little bubbles drift up from it now and then.
- Every tap target is at least 44px, and rail buttons are 38–58px.
- Each rail button has its own colour (Feed orange, Asma pink, Facts yellow, Stickers green, Memories purple, Settings blue), so kids can find them before they can read.
- Cards and panels spring in and fade out, toasts slide in, and rail icons float gently. Everything respects "reduce motion".
- Safe areas (notches, home bars) are respected, double-tap zoom is disabled, and floating bubbles show icons only on small screens to keep the ocean uncluttered.
- On phones, feeding, tapping a fish and the treasure chest give a tiny haptic buzz.
- **Full screen** button in Settings, and the app can be added to the home screen (web app manifest) to play like a real app.

## Getting started

Requirements: [Node.js](https://nodejs.org/) **20.19+** (the project pins 22.23.4 in `.nvmrc`).

```bash
nvm use            # or: nvm use 22.23.4
npm install
npm run dev        # open http://localhost:5173
```

| Script            | What it does                              |
| ----------------- | ----------------------------------------- |
| `npm run dev`     | Start the dev server with hot reload      |
| `npm run build`   | Build the production bundle into `dist/`  |
| `npm run preview` | Serve the production build locally        |

The `dist/` folder is a static site, so it can be hosted anywhere (GitHub Pages, Netlify, any web server).

## How to play

1. Tap **Feed**, or tap anywhere in the water, to give the fish snacks.
2. Tap a fish to say hi and see how it is doing.
3. Feed about 3–4 pellets per fish. They will tell you when they are full.
4. Come back regularly. Hungry fish get weak, and very hungry fish can die.
5. Grow your fish to unlock colors and babies, and tap the treasure chest for surprises.
6. Tap **👧 Asma** to invite her for a swim with your fish.
7. When the shark comes, tap it 3 times to protect your fish!
8. Tap the sea creatures to learn about them and fill your 📖 sticker book.
9. Tap the ☀️/🌙 badge to see the tank at sunset or night.

## Game timing

All timings live at the top of `src/state/store.js` and are easy to tune:

| Rule | Default |
| ---- | ------- |
| Fish get fully hungry (app open) | ~3 minutes |
| Starving while the app is open, until death | ~10 minutes |
| Fish get fully hungry (app closed) | ~30 minutes |
| Starving while the app is closed, until death | ~12 hours |
| Asma's breath underwater | ~32 seconds |
| Asma's rest before she can dive again | 20 seconds |
| First shark visit / later visits | ~75 seconds / every 2.5–4 minutes |
| Shark stays (unless chased away) | ~45 seconds |
| First big visitor / later visitors | ~60 seconds / every 3–5 minutes |
| Pellets to fill a tummy | ~3 |
| Pellets to grow from baby to adult | ~20 |

## Technical notes

- **Performance:** shared geometries and materials, instanced bubbles and hearts, and pooled pellets. Animation is clamped by frame time, and pixel ratio is capped at 1.5 and lowered automatically on slow devices (drei `PerformanceMonitor`). There are no shadows.
- **No re-render per frame:** fish motion lives in a mutable simulation (`src/sim`). React only re-renders on game events and a 1-second tick.
- **Resource cleanup:** geometries and materials created by components are disposed on unmount.
- **Responsive:** the swim area and camera adapt to portrait phones, tablets and desktops.

## Water, light and smoothness

- `src/scene/underwater.js` adds the moving light ripples and the "red fades with distance" water colour to every lit material, through one shared shader hook. A material with its own `onBeforeCompile` calls `THREE.Material.prototype.onBeforeCompile.call(material, shader)` first.
- The sand shader (`Seabed.jsx`) draws a soft shadow under each pet fish, the crab and the octopus.
- Fish lean into turns and their tail sweep follows their speed (`fishBrain.js`, `Fish.jsx`, `FishSchool.jsx`).
- `StaticBatch.jsx` merges meshes that never move into one mesh per material (used for the corals).
- In dev mode, `window.__ocean.gl.info.render` shows draw calls and triangles.
